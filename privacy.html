OTTO Health privacy policy

Who uses it. OTTO Health is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What it accesses. Read-only Google Health API permissions for activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements, and sleep. It cannot create, change or delete any data.

Where data goes. Data is copied to the owner's private server and stays there. It is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

How long. Until the owner deletes it.

Revoking access. Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy. OTTO Health's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact. The developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
